About

I build robots end to end: the mechanism, the embedded control, and the software that runs them.

I study mechanical engineering at Boston University with a concentration in Robotics & Technology Innovation and a minor in Business Administration & Management. I took aerospace coursework at UC Davis before transferring to BU in 2024.

I joined Prof. Keith Brown's KABLab in October 2024, my first semester at BU. Since then I've worked on BEAR-DEN, a self-driving lab for polymer research. I'm now on the lab's MAP Hub expansion, and I lead the ARCTOS team in the BU Robotics Club.

I'm applying to robotics Ph.D. programs to start in Fall 2027.

Research interests

  • Estimation and control
  • Autonomy for science and exploration
