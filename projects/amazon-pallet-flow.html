TL;DR

I was a Robotics Fulfillment Area Manager Intern at a robotics fulfillment center that handles more than 1M units a day. I led 50 to 100 associates against safety, productivity, and quality KPIs.

I led a DMAIC project that traced recurring stow blockers to pallet tracking errors. I designed a pallet building station to fix it, built a live dashboard, and presented the plan to senior site leadership in a white paper.
